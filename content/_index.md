Welcome to The Freudian Couch. I dabble in fiction, literary and personal essays.

I believe that reading is a subjective experience. Hence, my essays on books rarely tell if a book is good. They focus on my reading journey and identify what worked for me and what didn't. 

My fiction leaves a lot unsaid, hoping that readers fill in the gaps.

My essays meander and make non-existent connections. They don't judge, they examine. They don't always conclude, but they question.

I have been writing here since 2009, failing and learning in public. If you're looking for hot takes or productivity hacks, this isn't the place for you. But if you are looking to engage closely with literature, join in. Explore around, and see what you like, if anything. Let me know what works and what doesn't.
